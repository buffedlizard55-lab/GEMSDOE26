# Generative-AI assistance disclosure

An Arena.ai coding/research agent assisted with literature review, source classification,
experimental design, software, testing, and documentation. Predictions come from numerically
trained models and documented deterministic transforms, not invented geological labels.
The assistance covered most new implementation, tests, website text, source/claim ledgers,
and the interpretation of results. All reported H26 measurements were generated by executed
Python/JavaScript code on the real hash-pinned owner-mirrored assets; no fault labels, hidden
scores, geological vent confirmations or successful experiments were fabricated. The numerical
model is a small convolutional masked autoencoder with separately trained frozen-feature heads,
not a Prithvi/ViT replication or a generative language model assigning geological truth.

No hidden test labels were accessed, no account credentials were requested, and no competition
submission was uploaded by the agent. The primary FAILED its frozen performance gate; its TIFF
is research-only, BLOCKED_DO_NOT_SUBMIT. A cold fixed-parameter replication reproduced the
failure and file bytes exactly; it was not a new independent confirmation or tuning attempt. Human competitors remain responsible for eligibility,
accuracy, authorship representations, licensing and the final contest entry.

The official September 2026 rules, §3.2, require indicating the extent and use of generative AI
in the submission narrative (outside its word count):
