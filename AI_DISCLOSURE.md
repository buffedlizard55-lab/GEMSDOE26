# Generative-AI assistance disclosure

An Arena.ai coding/research agent assisted with literature review, source classification,
experimental design, software, testing, and documentation. Predictions come from numerically
trained models and documented deterministic transforms, not invented geological labels.
No hidden test labels were accessed, no account credentials were requested, and no competition
submission was uploaded by the agent. Human competitors remain responsible for eligibility,
accuracy, authorship representations, licensing and the final contest entry.

The official September 2026 rules, §3.2, require indicating the extent and use of generative AI
in the submission narrative (outside its word count):
https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf

This disclosure is not an eligibility certification or legal advice. Include/update it in any
required finalist narrative and reproducibility documentation.
