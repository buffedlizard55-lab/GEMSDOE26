Why H25 can gain without new geology

H25 is a deterministic, label-free subset of H19-5: 121,131 → 60,069 cells. We independently reproduced its BFS/geodesic 1.5-pixel thinning, exactly. It preserves local support around parent points, but can reduce weighted credit to unknown truth.

w(d) = max(1 − d / 300 m, 0)
T = sum over truth of max nearby w(d) × prediction
F = sum over predictions of prediction × (1 − max nearby truth w(d))
G = number of truth pixels
DTI = T / [0.2(T + F) + 0.8G]

T+F is not generally the emitted count. Nearby redundant predictions do not add credit to a truth pixel once its maximum is reached; each still contributes separate FP mass. The older “each emitted pixel costs 0.2” simplification is only approximately true when FP dominates.

Let thinning retain r of T and c of F. Score improves precisely when 0.2(r − c)F > 0.8(1 − r)G. Our paired dense values retain 87.16% T but only 49.44% F; local sparse DTI increases 0.071466 → 0.096261. The reported hidden-score increase 0.1922 → 0.2477 is consistent with this mechanism, but lacks an authenticated file receipt or hidden T/F decomposition.

Old H25 fold-mean 0.1764 is not our primary pooled dense 0.171825. In fact our independently recomputed fold mean is 0.176454, matching its rounded historical value. Pooled and fold means must stay labelled.

What could beat 0.2477 / 0.3195?

No validated new candidate here does. The strongest reported owner file remains H25. Global confidence down-scaling cannot improve this metric at fixed support: with G>0, DTI(c)=cT/[0.2c(T+F)+0.8G] increases with c. More thinning cannot discover a missed structure; broad halos can be expensive.

At score s, additions with positive δF need δT/δF > 0.2s/(1−0.2s). At 0.2477, that is about 0.05212 weighted TP per weighted FP. This is algebra, not an estimated hit rate. The target needs better geological information at a controlled budget—not a private-density assumption inverted from one lattice score.

H26-XEDGE has now tested the cross-scale edge hypothesis and failed its frozen gate; do not retune the revealed folds. Next priorities are independent untouched spatial/external fault truth, original feature semantics and official native-data verification. Only then should a fresh slate be ranked and preregistered. H26-DRAIN remains conditional on actual official bytes/coverage. A random-frozen-encoder control is needed only for a separately justified new SSL study.

Fault discovery ≠ confirmed geothermal resources

Structural permeability alone is insufficient. Useful geothermal discovery requires heat and fluid evidence, stress-compatible permeability, reservoir/lithology constraints and eventually field/well validation. Faults may be conduits or barriers. Blind Southern Gabbs Valley case studies justify integrating gravity/magnetics/MT under cover, not declaring every magnetic low a vent.
