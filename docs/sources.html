Irregularities that remain open

  • The mirror example has 60,988 ones and exactly equals known labels. It is not the absence sample described by the organizer. Its pixels were not used in label-free pretraining; labels were opened only after the SSL, XEDGE and DILCOND features were frozen.
  • tc and depth aliases are unresolved. GDR defines thermal conductivity for its own table; this does not prove the meaning of the bridge band. All numeric bands were retained without silently asserting physical units. The official reference-solution repository corroborates the band-tag schema (see sources/band_tags_official.csv), not the physical meaning of ambiguous short codes.
  • Acquisition-block polygons and road/claim distances are owner-derived; their hashes were checked. These are nuisance diagnostics, not official-coordinate geometry or causal fault evidence.
  • 3DEP, USGS ANSS earthquake-catalog and AWS Open Data endpoints all have real, documented public APIs, but direct binary/API HTTPS requests fail (SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL) from this sandbox — see evidence/egress_check.json. Native-DEM drainage analysis, the raw seismic-lineament hypothesis and the Sentinel-2 alteration hypothesis are therefore not yet viable executable candidates here, though all three sources are confirmed free and official in principle.
  • Initial DrivenData review used tools before its automatic-access prohibition was found. No later scraper, score monitor or uploader is installed; the target remains a dated snapshot.
  • Older sibling deadline clocks and “unscored” H25 banners conflict with later reports. Confirm organizer deadline/rules at eventual human entry; never silently pick an unauthenticated clock.
