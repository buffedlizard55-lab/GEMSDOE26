## Decision and scientific hypotheses

Rank is **ordinal expected incremental DTI benefit × feasibility**, not a fabricated numerical ΔDTI. All effects are unknown until measured. Novelty means relative to the reviewed public sibling code/sites (GEMSDOE24, 19GEMSDOE, GEMSDOE10, 16GEMSDOE, 20GEMSDOE, GEMSDOE22/23), not priority over the scientific literature or unseen competitors.

## Read primary sources

- MAE, He et al., CVPR 2022: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/He_Masked_Autoencoders_Are_Scalable_Vision_Learners_CVPR_2022_paper.html — random patches reconstructed, 75% example masking, asymmetric ViT. Our small **convolutional masked autoencoder is inspired by this objective, not an architectural reproduction**.
- Prithvi, Jakubik et al., 2023: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18660 — multispectral HLS pretraining and small-labelled-set fine-tuning. Does not establish that reconstruction errors locate Nevada faults or that HLS weights suit geophysical channels.
- Earney et al., 2018 (USGS authors; DOE OSTI): https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1724114 — blind Gabbs Valley structures constrained by gravity, magnetics and MT; lithologic contrasts and alteration are competing explanations.
- Hermant et al., 2025, Stanford workshop: https://pangea.stanford.edu/ERE/db/GeoConf/papers/SGW/2025/Hermant.pdf — faults may create topographic changes; catalogue heterogeneity and local offsets up to 400 m in another mapped Nevada area. Not a measurement of this competition's hidden labels.
- Siler & Faulds, DOE OSTI: https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1110515 — heat and permeability must be collocated; fault tips/intersections are favourable but faults are not confirmed geothermal vents.
- GeoDAWN, USGS: https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/657e1d85d34e23d3533209f7 — four acquisition blocks, east–west flight lines 200/400 m apart, variable terrain clearance. These are nuisance hypotheses, not geological evidence.
- 3DEP availability query: https://tnmaccess.nationalmap.gov/api/v1/products?bbox=-119.5,39.5,-119.4,39.6&datasets=Digital%20Elevation%20Model%20%28DEM%29%201%20meter&max=2 — returned actual official download URLs, sizes and public-domain statement; see source ledger for exact URLs.

## Frozen experiment H26-SSL-v1

1. **Labels are not opened until pretraining and masked-error inference complete.** The only spatial footprint is the label-free run-length mask, not template prediction values. The template is used later for format comparison only. Merely downloading/hashing label bytes is not label-based training.
2. Prepare all 19 bands over the complete footprint. Per-band robust scale from unlabeled pixels only. Missing-value masks are explicit, never training targets. No reconstruction of sentinel nodata. Do not downsample the raster to a few labelled patches.
3. CPU-sized convolutional encoder, random 4×4-pixel patches, 75% masking, six complete tile passes. Every footprint pixel is visited; the last pass forces previously unmasked patches to receive a masked target. Keep measured exposure and target coverage counts and encoder checksum. No coordinates, catalogue distances, roads, claims or block IDs are representation-training inputs.
4. Freeze encoder and decoder. Compute clean embeddings and **masked**, not self-copy, reconstruction error using complementary masks and overlapping inference halos. Produce a separate label-free error map. Normalize/cohere it without labels. A high error is **not** independently validated as a fault.
5. Only then load labels. Four outer spatial quadrants as in GEMSDOE24 (median row/column of footprint); 1.5 km train/test exclusion collar, exclude training positives belonging to any connected component touching the test quadrant. Do not mask held-out positives from the OOF prediction. Fit only a small final neural classification head; encoder parameters and checksum cannot change.
6. **Matched supervised control:** same head capacity, seeds and training samples on standardized raw bands + explicit missingness + the same owner-derived lidar terrain descriptors. **SSL head:** those same features + frozen embedding + masked errors. This is not a mathematically unbiased PU estimator: unlabelled far cells are imperfect negative examples. Document this selection bias.
7. Primary fixed candidate = 90% percentile-rank SSL head + 10% percentile-rank coherent label-free error. Ablations = raw head, SSL head without error fusion, error alone. Do not change this mixture after seeing outer-fold scores. No hyperparameter/budget sweep on confirmation.
8. Fixed emission budget **60,069 / 5,167,373** of eligible footprint per quadrant, score-ordered dot spacing ≥1.5 pixels. Same operator for matched heads. Full inference before fold cropping preserves halos. Exact float32 output, binary confidence 0/1, NaN outside.
9. Re-score the **exact pinned H25-1 D1.5 file** (reported 0.2477) and its H19-5 parent as emitted, under these same local evaluation masks. These are historical diagnostics, not leakage-free retrained controls: their all-catalogue exclusion mask had access to held-out trace locations. Do not relabel inherited scores as new OOF validation.
10. Dense known-fault DTI plus 20%-component sparse truth simulations: 30 paired selection draws and 30 disjoint-seed confirmation draws, per quadrant. Compute both pooled-component DTI and fold means; pooled DTI is primary. No hidden-label density is taken as fact from a single lattice score.
11. **Promotion necessary conditions:** primary beats the current historical D1.5 comparator AND the matched raw head by ≥0.005 pooled sparse DTI on selection AND confirmation, wins at least 3/4 confirmation quadrants against each, and pooled dense DTI is not more than 0.005 worse than either. Must have valid output and complete coverage receipts. Four regions are not 120 independent geological experiments: draws share the same faults; no nominal significance claim.
12. Exact-file nuisance diagnostic: roads, closed mining claims and the four **derived, not official-coordinate** acquisition blocks, with label audit preceding prediction audit. Compare accessibility association to the pinned reference. This is diagnostic association, not causal identification. Missing nuisance inputs or larger association by >0.01 blocks promotion. Report error dependence on missingness, patch phase and acquisition blocks; no post-hoc repair in this confirmatory run.
13. Failure = retain all results, generate and clearly mark the TIFF **research-only, do not submit**, and spend no weekly slot. A format pass is not a scientific release pass. No autonomous upload under any outcome. Never claim >0.2477 or >0.3195 without a platform result linked to the file.

## Restrictions and evidence boundary

The whole-raster representation fit is **transductive**: it sees unlabelled features of holdout geography. All label-based fitting remains geographically blocked. The sparse simulation is a catalogue-density sensitivity analysis, not independent unknown-fault truth. The current historical best cannot be converted into a clean OOF detector from its emitted binary raster. Additional independent validation is needed for novel-fault discovery and Phase 2.

DrivenData Terms of Use explicitly prohibit automated site access. An initial requested source/leaderboard review was performed before this restriction was discovered; it was a tool-retrieved snapshot, **not a human-read snapshot**. No further automated DrivenData access, recurring scraping, authentication or upload will be implemented. Permitted source APIs may refresh automatically. Prize rules PDF read through its final section; three submissions per week, one selected final entry, eligibility verification, full reproducibility assets and AI-use disclosure are requirements.

This preregistration must be committed before implementation/run. Amendments must be explicit, dated and cannot retroactively qualify a failed run.
