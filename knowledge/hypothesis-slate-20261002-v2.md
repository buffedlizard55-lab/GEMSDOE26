### Source-backed limits on interpretation

- The [USGS GeoDAWN ScienceBase release](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/657e1d85d34e23d3533209f7) describes an airborne magnetic/radiometric survey and coordinated lidar, and documents four acquisition blocks, differing flight specifications and variable terrain clearance. It does not authenticate the 19 competition-bridge channel aliases or their units.
- The public [DOE/GDR INGENIOUS catalog](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) lists geodetic shear/dilation models, seismicity, magnetics, gravity and conductance among its own regional datasets. This does not establish that any specific raster band in the owner mirror is one of those products.
- USGS's [Southern Gabbs Valley blind-system study](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1724114) reports a particular thermal anomaly near faults bounding an intra-basin gravity high, with a coincident conductive zone and magnetic low. The paper treats alteration as an interpretation and integrates independent field measurements; it does not prove a universal single-layer fault signature.
- The competition objective is **fault locations**, not confirmed vents or commercially viable geothermal systems. A fault prediction alone does not establish heat, fluid, permeability, or a geothermal resource.

## Frozen H27-SRCOH-v1 screen (committed before implementation/evaluation)

1. Prepare all 19 aligned feature bands and complete the existing six-pass whole-footprint masked pretraining, frozen representation inference and label-free anomaly calculation **before any label-pixel read**. H27 feature generation receives only prepared values, observed masks and the footprint; it must not import/open labels or template prediction values. In this numeric-only screen no coordinate, label, template-value, claim, road, event, external DEM or fault-distance feature enters H27-SRCOH.
2. Use only zero-based input bands `(3, 6, 7)` as named above, their explicit observed masks and the feature-grid footprint. Support is their joint observation/footprint mask eroded by 48 pixels (four times the largest σ) with a 3×3 all-true structure. Missing values are not targets or edges.
3. For each fixed σ ∈ `{3, 6, 12}` pixels, use SciPy Gaussian filtering with `mode="nearest"` and `truncate=4.0`; compute row and column derivatives directly with the corresponding first-order Gaussian-filter orders, in pixel units, so the 48-pixel erosion covers the full filter support. Rank each gradient magnitude over support with average ties divided by the support count. A pairwise alignment is `abs(gᵢ·gⱼ)/(||gᵢ||·||gⱼ|| + 1e-6)`, clipped to `[0,1]`, and is zero if either gradient norm is `≤1e-12`. At each scale, combine the three magnitude ranks and three pairwise alignments as `exp(mean(log(term + 1e-6)))`. For each channel, use unit row/column gradients to compute doubled-angle cross-scale orientation persistence `R = hypot(mean(cos(2θ)), mean(sin(2θ)))`; a zero-gradient vector contributes `(0,0)`. Combine scales as `exp(mean(log(scale_score + 1e-6)))`, and multiply by `(R₀R₁R₂)^(1/3)` with no floor on the persistence product, so a zero-persistence channel forces zero raw score. Percentile-rank on support using average ties divided by support count; force exact zero raw scores to ranked zero so tied non-edges cannot become an artificial average-rank pseudo-signal. No tuning, threshold search, alternate band selection or post-hoc feature edit.
4. Save code/protocol/input/feature hashes and the feature receipt before the first label-pixel access. The candidate fit adds exactly one ranked feature to the 51-dimension raw head. Its control receives a constant-zero 52nd column so first-layer/head parameter counts match; both use the same per-fold seed and epochs. The encoder and decoder remain frozen. Outputs are ranking scores, not calibrated probabilities.
5. Use the existing four NW/NE/SW/SE spatial quadrants, 1.5-km train/test collar and whole-connected-component exclusion. Use the existing sample caps and 16-epoch head schedule. Emit each fold with the frozen 60,069/5,167,373 global budget fraction, the same ridge NMS and 1.5-pixel Poisson spacing. Use draw offsets 120–149 for the prespecified screen and 150–179 for its seed-offset repeat; those are resampling draws over the same catalogue, not independent truth.
6. Compare the candidate against (a) the same-run dimension-matched raw head, (b) the fixed H26-XEDGE OOF raster as emitted, and (c) the exact historical H25-1 raster as emitted, explicitly retaining H25's all-catalogue-mask leakage. The H27 candidate must improve mean pooled sparse DTI by at least 0.005 over **each** comparator in both draw groups, win at least 3/4 spatial quadrants against each in the repeat, and be no worse than any comparator by more than 0.005 pooled dense DTI. Report component draw variation and each fold, not only pooled means.
7. A failed screen remains `BLOCKED_DO_NOT_SUBMIT`; a passing reused-catalogue screen permits only a separate, preregistered, genuinely untouched/external confirmation. No file is uploaded and no weekly slot is used by this screen. No local proxy result is a claim of >0.2477 or >0.3195 leaderboard score.
